Automated speech benchmark (no microphone needed)

Each command is spoken by a text-to-speech model (SpeechT5) in several voices, including an Indian-accented English voice, optionally with background noise. The audio then goes through the app's own Whisper loader (speech.js) and parser (commands.js), and is also scored with the original hackathon parser for comparison. Synthetic speech is cleaner than a real person talking, so treat these numbers as an upper bound.

URL options: ?model=tiny (fast model), ?set=heldout (24 commands never used to tune the parser), ?voices=indian,us_female, ?limit=5 (first N prompts, for a quick check).
